💡 Sizing Tips

  • • Sizes may vary slightly between different brands
  • • Measure yourself in the morning for accuracy
  • • If between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort
  • • Shoes may feel snug initially but typically stretch with wear
  • • For custom orders, provide exact measurements

📏 How to Measure

  1. Shoes: Measure from heel to toe in centimeters when barefoot
  2. Chest: Measure across your chest at the fullest point, keeping tape parallel to ground
  3. Waist: Measure at your natural waist, keeping tape comfortable (not tight)
  4. Length: For shirts, measure from shoulder to desired length
