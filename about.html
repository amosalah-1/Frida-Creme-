Our Story

FRIDAH CREME BALES started with a simple mission: to make quality shoes and clothing accessible and affordable for everyone in Muranga. We believe that great style shouldn't come with a hefty price tag.

Founded in 2020, we've grown from a small local shop to a trusted name in the community. Our commitment to quality, fair pricing, and excellent customer service has earned us the loyalty of hundreds of satisfied customers.

Today, we continue to curate premium footwear and apparel collections, offering something for everyone—from casual everyday wear to formal occasions.
